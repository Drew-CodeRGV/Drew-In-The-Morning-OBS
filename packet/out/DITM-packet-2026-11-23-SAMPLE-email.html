'6-7'
A catchphrase kids yell for laughs, often with a hands-weighing gesture. No fixed meaning; some link it to LaMelo Ball (6'7"). Already being mocked as overused.
How to bring it up: Laugh and ask them to show you the gesture; it's meant to confuse adults.

'Pennywide' meme
A Halloween trick-or-treat video of a child in a Pennywise mask went viral and became memes mocking him. A cruelty meme aimed at a real kid, remixed into brainrot edits.

🎓 High School

The Door Kick Challenge [CONFIRM BEFORE AIR: No RGV incident found; X shows only ~39 posts in 7 days]
A TikTok trend: kick or pound a stranger's front door, film it, run. Police say it can mean vandalism, trespass or burglary charges, and a scared homeowner may react.
How to bring it up: It's a crime, not a prank; Irvine PD says parents can be held accountable. Ask what they'd do if friends filmed it.

Slang: aura, aura farming, cooked
'Aura' = someone's vibe; 'aura farming' = doing things to look cool online; 'cooked' = in trouble. Words to question: 'huzz,' 'bop,' 'big back' can objectify or mock women and bodies.
How to bring it up: Be curious, not corrective: ask what a word means. Terms burn out fast.
